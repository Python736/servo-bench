How it works

  • Drag a servo to move it. Drop it on an orange dot to plug it onto that arm.
  • Drag a white horn to turn the servo’s shaft.
  • Arrows move it precisely, rings (Rotate) spin it. Arrow keys nudge.
  • Drag empty space to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
